Of the 2024–2025 matched CISO generator identities, **42** changed reported EIA-860 status. The unit-level audit reconciles their OP nameplate contribution to **+862.4 MW**. **None of the 42 records changed its reported operating month or year** between annual snapshots.

All 3 pumped-hydro units at plant 6100 (generator IDs 1, 2, 3) retain **June 1984** as reported operating date, with +351 MW OP each. See [pumped-storage unit audit](EIA860_CISO_PUMPED_STORAGE_STATUS_AUDIT_2024_2025.md).

## Interpretation and reconciliation

- The annual OP nameplate fleet increased **+6,895.8 MW** overall. The status-changed subset contributes **+862.4 MW** (approximately **12.5%** of the net change); the other classification components net **+6,033.4 MW**.
- A change in annual EIA-860 status is **not** evidence of newly built capacity or independently verified commissioning. Reported operating dates are unchanged, but that alone does not establish physical availability, ownership, BA transfer or the reason for status changes.
- **OA, OP, OS and SB are retained as raw EIA codes** until definitions are independently checked against the applicable official codebook. OP nameplate is not hourly firm supply.
- This analysis does not approve empirical PyPSA dispatch. Hourly renewable and thermal availability, storage constraints, costs and interchange are still required.

## Reproduction

The complete unit-level records and reconciliation assertions are generated by `src/multiyear_energy_validation/eia860_transitions.py`, written to the CI artifact `fleet_transitions_2024_2025.json` as `status_change_unit_records` and included in the workflow summary. Official source snapshots are downloaded by the CI workflow; source data are not committed to the repository.
