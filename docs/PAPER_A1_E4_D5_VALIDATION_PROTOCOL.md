# D5 prospective temporal validation protocol
Freeze before inspecting new window scores. Use first 168 UTC hours of February, May, August, November in 2024 and 2025, 8 windows. July and January/April/October are development windows, not holdouts. Same annual modeled fleet for W0/W2 and PV0/PV2, no monthly scalar extrapolation. W2 alpha 1/7; PV2 losses 0, .20, .25 fixed from development. No BA-specific tuning or time shifts. Exact UTC raw EIA930 observations, report missing pairs and capacity coverage; compare models on identical hours. Report equal BA-window mean NRMSE, worst group, bias, MAE, correlation, paired wins. No independent future-year claim. Monthly snapshot sensitivity remains separate. Candidate admission requires review of systematic bias, coverage, radiation time semantics and reporting scope; no automatic admission based on rank.
